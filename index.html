Message

From classrooms and corridors to careers and milestones — the journey we began in 1976 has been nothing short of extraordinary. This 50th reunion is not just a gathering; it’s a celebration of friendships, memories, and the spirit of IIT Kanpur that has stayed with us through the decades. Let’s come together to reconnect, look back on the moments that shaped us, and return to the place where it all started -IITK. As we mark this golden milestone, we also have a chance to give back to the institution that shaped our lives. Together, we’re aiming to create a meaningful legacy with a heartfelt goal of ₹10 crore - a contribution that reflects our gratitude for all that IITK gave us.
